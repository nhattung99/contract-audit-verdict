Title: ContractAuditVerdict - On-Chain Audit Report Quality

Description:

ContractAuditVerdict is a reusable on-chain audit-report quality primitive. It reads a report and audited source, scores five technical criteria, and records a deterministic grade with coverage gaps.

GenLayer validators independently fetch and assess the evidence. The custom validator agrees on the MEANING of the decision, not JSON format: exact grade agreement, overall within 5, every criterion within 10, matching confidence bands, and gaps on both sides for SUPERFICIAL.

The assessment and lookup API supports DAO audit acceptance gates, protocol due diligence, and auditor reputation tracking. This standalone Intelligent Contract has no frontend or escrow. It ships with 54 passing tests and documentation, and is deployed on studionet at 0x695f6609d680A5cF1A0a2b283f63aB6176879206.
